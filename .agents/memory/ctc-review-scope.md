---
name: CTC review scope
description: The intended facility-based coverage of CTC In-Charge approvals.
---

CTC In-Charge reviews the CHW, Health Care Provider and Data Clerk work logs for the assigned facility, covering both Services and Payroll. Separate cadre grants are used for technical review, not to omit facility cadres from CTC review.

Facility In-Charge endorsement is removed from the approval workflow. Preserve historical endorsements, but do not reintroduce that prerequisite or replace it with CTC removal.

**Why:** The user asked to confirm that CTC In-Charge can see their facility's cadres. They subsequently asked to remove Facility In-Charge endorsement, not CTC verification.

**How to apply:** Keep facility scope enforced on lists, direct record access and verification actions. Keep the Services and Payroll queues separate. Do not replace facility-based CTC coverage with technical-review cadre restrictions without an explicit policy change.
